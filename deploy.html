Deploy ProofRegistry

Deploys contracts/ProofRegistry.sol (solc 0.8.24 · evmVersion paris · optimizer off — the settings on the footer certificate) from your MetaMask account. Testnet first, then mainnet.

Ready. You will confirm one transaction in MetaMask.
  1. After deploying, open /app on this same localhost address (add ?net=testnet for testnet) — it picks up the new address automatically.
  2. For the live site, paste the address into registry: '' in assets/config.js and into the README.
  3. Then verify the registry with Verify Buddy itself: open /app?address=<address> and drop contracts/ProofRegistry.sol.
